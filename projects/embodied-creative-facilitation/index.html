Embodied Creative Facilitation

Epistemic peer-to-peer collaboration.

Choreographic Coding Lab · Digital Bodies

I create contexts for collaboration, and for exploring how different kinds of cognitive assemblages meet computational technologies: scores, rule systems, and interactive systems that can use AI, XR, and AR. The emphasis is the collaboration itself, an epistemic peer-to-peer environment.

I mainly do this yearly. What is presented here are the contexts I have been invited to facilitate: the Choreographic Coding Lab, and Digital Bodies at Lake Studios Berlin. The facilitation can begin in Vipassana meditation and move into an experiment with choreographic practice, improvisational games, and the uses of cognitive media and AI in the creative process. It draws on different kinds of knowledge mapping.

This kind of context draws on my research into network environments and open spaces for creativity and collaboration. That research was deeply influenced by the philosophical framework of HZT Berlin, where I was a research associate from 2012 to 2016.

Choreographic Coding Lab

The Choreographic Coding Lab is a traveling laboratory founded by Motion Bank. Since 2013 it has moved between cities. I facilitated the last two editions in the UK, invited by Aoi Nakamura and Esteban Lecoq of A+E Lab, their art and technology hub in Chatham, Kent.

Royal Ballet and Opera

The Choreographic Coding Lab at the Royal Ballet and Opera took place May 30–June 7, 2026, as part of the RBO/SHIFT festival. The public sharing was on June 7 at PLAY SHIFT. Participants worked inside the house, across costumes, archives, and technical production. I facilitated it with Anton Koch (Motion Bank) and Daniel Bisig (Zurich University of the Arts). Aoi Nakamura and Esteban Lecoq of AΦE / A+E Lab hosted. The lab was commissioned by the Royal Ballet and Opera, with support from ElevenLabs.

A+E Lab, Chatham

The second International Choreographic Coding Lab at A+E Lab ran March 24–28, 2025, in the Historic Dockyard, Chatham, with a public sharing on March 28. This was the lab’s second edition in the UK, and its focus was artificial intelligence. I facilitated it with David Rittershaus (Motion Bank, Hochschule Mainz) and Daniel Bisig (Centre for Dance Research, Coventry University, Zurich University of the Arts, and Motion Bank). Aoi Nakamura and Esteban Lecoq hosted the week.

The gathering opened the evening before, on March 23, with XR Sunday Social at A+E Lab.

Digital Bodies

Digital Bodies is the framework I facilitate at Lake Studios Berlin, and I mainly do it yearly. In 2026 I co-facilitate Digital Body 4.0, 8–22 November, a residency for Berlin artists, with Mark Coniglio.
