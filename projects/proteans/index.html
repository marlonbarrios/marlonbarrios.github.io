Proteans

“There is always someone for you… in the future”

Single-channel video installation · Concept, programming, and AI video production

Proteans plays a continuous loop of ten time travelers from the future, each seeking to date people from our present or their past. In each video, a Protean introduces themselves and shares their story.

The imagery and text for each Protean are generated through Proteus, an app I developed. A large language model writes the text. DALL-E makes the images. Deepfake then brings those stills to life with the corresponding text.
