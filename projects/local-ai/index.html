Local AI

Research into models that run on the machine.

The apps live within Laboratory of Synthetic Meaning

Local AI is research into running language models on the machine in front of you.

The model stays with the person who runs it. The work does not depend on a remote service remaining available. The code can be shared, read, and changed.

So far the apps are My LatentWelt, Semantic Tensegrities, I Am Not Saying, Machine Forgetting, and El Inmigrante. They live within Laboratory of Synthetic Meaning. Bauhaus Time Traveler is the local AI version of that app, and part of Born in Latent Space.

The ontology is part of this research. A local model can read the map of the work.
