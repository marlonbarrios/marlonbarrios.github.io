Projects

  • Hybrid Intelligences

    A three-year research project and hybrid tool for dynamic knowledge. I launched it in summer 2026 at the University of Florida. Intelligence is treated as a relational event across bodies, tools, and environments.

  • Born in Latent Space

    A lecture-performance, installation, and workshop platform, 2024–2029. Voice, movement, storytelling, and generative AI co-compose thinking in real time.

  • UnstableLandscapes

    A procedural ecology I have been developing since 1997. Performances, installations, and lecture-experiments in which cognition is distributed across bodies, codes, and environments.

  • UnstableLandscapes, 2003–2008

    Interactive performances I programmed mostly in Max/MSP/Jitter. Improvisational dance, interactivity, and real-time processing.

  • The Tectonics of Otherness

    A residency and installation I initiated with María Luisa Angulo during Beyond Gravity at Theater im Depot, Dortmund, October 2025. A synthetic media space.

  • Dark Enlightenment

    A rave within synthetic media. Techno-republics and Other Vectors, at Theater im Depot, Dortmund, October 2025. AI-generated songs and videos, reactive light, and fog.

  • Tangerine Dreams

    An installation. I presented the prototype in January 2026 at Afrofuturism Week, produced by CAME at the Cade. A version with an open-source language model premieres at the Cade Museum in April 2027.

  • Interactive Generative Videos

    Single-channel interactive videos. Placed together in a room, as an assembly, they are what I call a stable landscape.

  • Proteans

    A single-channel video installation. Ten time travelers from the future, each seeking to date someone from our present or their past. Imagery and text made in the app Proteus.

  • Choreographic Coding Lab

    I facilitated the last two Choreographic Coding Labs in the UK, invited by Aoi Nakamura and Esteban Lecoq of A+E Lab: Chatham, March 2025, and the Royal Ballet and Opera, 2026.

  • Laboratory of Synthetic Meaning

    A series of minimal apps I have been making since October 2025. Gesture, face, real-time voice, and text, with language models used as material and drawn as form in the browser.

  • Synthetic Unconscious

    Begun in October 2026. Thematic Apperception and Rorschach images given to language and video models, in dialogue with research on model introspection.

  • Time Instances

    A computational installation. Twenty screens, each running a live generative system in the browser. Minimal geometry, a shared sound field, and procedures that perform time.

  • In Pursuit of Stolen Ghosts

    Duet in Latent Space. I developed it during a residency at Lake Studios Berlin in February 2024, on cultural loss, diaspora, and Venezuelan heritage.

  • Digressions in Latent Space

    An electronic experimental album made with recording tools and generative AI. It runs alongside the lecture-performance Duets in Latent Space.

  • Diffusion

    Writings, press, media, and publications, including the Hybrid Intelligences essays and the BBWCXR catalog.

  • Lake Studios Berlin

    A dance research and residency center. I am a founding member. I designed how the residencies work, and I continue as researcher in residence.

  • Courses at UF

    I teach Algorithmic Creativity and AI, Art, and Society, asynchronous courses in the AI and the Arts Certificate at the College of the Arts.

  • Dance-tech Project

    In 2007 I started dance-tech.net, a social network for dance and movement working with digital technologies, and later movimiento.org. More than 200 video interviews.

Concepts

  • Latent space

    The unseeable field in which machine learning makes meaning. In the work it is also a poetic terrain for memory, resistance, and planetary futurities.

  • Cognitive assemblage

    After N. Katherine Hayles: a web of interdependent cognitive systems, human and nonhuman, in which cognition emerges from relations rather than from a single mind.

  • Complex embodiment

    Mind as a world-involving process. A living body is already a nested ecology of nerves, memory, culture, tools, rooms, and other bodies.

  • Synthetic cognition

    Computational creativity treated as a material. Language models and self-organizing processes become aesthetic agents, not only tools.

  • Epistemic fiction

    AI-generated scripts, poetic scores, and distorted prompts used as provocations. They open space for improvisation, ambiguity, and embodied interpretation.

  • Knowledge dramaturgies

    Performance-lectures, games, and game-like structures used as sites where cognition becomes participatory, unstable, and playful.

  • Stable landscape

    The name I use for an assembly of the interactive generative videos placed together in a room. Distinct from the project UnstableLandscapes.

  • Queer longing

    One of the frameworks of the work, with techno-intimacy, cybernetic feedback, migration, and diasporic memory.

  • Diasporic memory

    Memory, migration, and displacement as material. The Global South imagines otherwise without asking permission.

  • Speculative AI

    AI used not to predict the future but to liberate it. Speculative theory as compass and choreography against extractivist and disembodied optimization.

  • Bottom-up architectures

    Systems that evolve through local interactions rather than centralized control, drawn from biological ecologies and from Varela and Maturana.

  • Nonhuman cognition

    Machine imagination treated as a living speculative zone. Models hallucinate, fail, glitch, and speculate. Their outputs are provisional fragments.

  • Emptiness

    Śūnyatā, from Buddhist practice, used as epistemic clearing: the work stays open, decentered, and without a fixed point of authority.

  • Open source

    Works are published under open-source licenses as epistemic sharing and community care. Code on GitHub is released under the MIT License.
